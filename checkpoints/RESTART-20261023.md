## What the evidence establishes

The official evaluator checks are sampled. HHL's numeric model covers 2,031,616
inputs; the strict gate audit covers 71,424 selected cases (7,936 selector
classes, three lower-byte patterns and three accumulator choices). Coulomb's
integer oracle covers all 195,075 positive norms; its strict audit covers
837,822 selected cases (139,637 attainable norms, two coordinate orientations
and three accumulator choices). The affine ledger covers every measurement
outcome **on those tested basis inputs**. Whole-circuit checks still share
upstream simulator/model code.

Additional component checks cover the HHL suffix under its stated incoming
phase contract, the parity-alias interface under the baseline restoration
contract, and the lookup over all physical addresses, affine dirty outputs and
measurement outcomes. These are **not** complete all-coordinate/all-accumulator
proofs of the whole tasks. Earlier unavailable lower-metric sources and reports
have not been recovered and are not reproducible results of this checkpoint.
